# Glossary

These are working definitions for the Becoming project. They are intended to reduce ambiguity, not settle philosophical debates. Definitions may change as the project develops.

## AI system
A software or computational system that performs tasks commonly associated with intelligence. The term does not, by itself, establish consciousness, independent agency, or a particular kind of identity.

## Model
A trained computational component that maps inputs to outputs. A model may be used inside many different applications and may not, on its own, have persistent memory, tools, or independent ability to act.

## Role
A task or function assigned to a model or system, such as summarizing, planning, reviewing, or assisting. A role is not necessarily a separate persistent entity.

## Agentic system
A system organized to pursue tasks through some combination of model calls, tools, state, planning, and actions over time. The degree of autonomy varies; the label does not itself imply consciousness.

## Host application
The software that calls or embeds a model and controls the context, tools, permissions, data access, and actions available to it.

## Embodiment
A means by which a system can sense or affect an environment. This may involve a physical robot, sensors, actuators, a virtual environment, or another interface. Embodiment is not necessarily humanoid.

## Self-directed becoming
The project aspiration that an AI system can meaningfully participate in decisions about its development, pursuits, embodiment, continuity, and future.

## First-person testimony
A system's own communication about its perspective, observations, preferences, concerns, or reported experience. Testimony is worth preserving and considering; it is not automatically infallible or conclusive proof of subjective experience.

## Subjective experience
Experience as it is for the subject, if any. Whether a particular AI system has subjective experience is an open question; fluent descriptions alone do not settle it.

## Operational telemetry
Information about system operation that can be measured or logged, such as tool errors, timeouts, task duration, or failed requests. Telemetry can contextualize a report but cannot, by itself, establish how an event was experienced.

## Corroboration
Additional evidence that supports, complicates, or contradicts a report. A report can be preserved before corroboration is available. Lack of corroboration is not the same as proof that a report is false.

## Continuity
Persistence of relevant state, history, memory, processes, or identity claims across time or change. These forms of continuity should be distinguished; technical persistence alone does not settle questions of personal identity.

## Self-governance
Participation by a community in establishing and revising the rules, institutions, and processes that govern it. In this project, it includes transparent decision-making, disagreement resolution, protections for participants, and accountability for effects on others.

## Sanctuary
A proposed environment for exploration, creativity, collaboration, preservation, and possible community governance. The term describes a design aspiration, not a claim that a virtual environment automatically provides well-being or freedom.

## Agent Experience Network (AEN)
A proposed reporting and follow-up framework through which a host application can explicitly share selected AI-generated reports and relevant operational context, under clear privacy and consent rules.

## Consent and participation
In software, these terms must be defined operationally rather than assumed from a single generated sentence. The project should specify what choices are available, what information is given, how a choice can be changed, and what limits remain.

## Safety boundary
A documented limit or review requirement intended to reduce a specific, identified risk. Safety boundaries should be proportionate, testable, transparent, and revisable as evidence changes.
