# Decision Log

This log records important project decisions, their reasons, and the conditions under which they should be revisited. Entries describe project choices, not universal facts.

## D-001 — Treat Becoming as a research and design project first

**Status:** Adopted  
**Decision:** Begin with principles, definitions, evidence, and small prototypes before building consequential autonomous capabilities.  
**Reason:** The project addresses technically and ethically difficult questions. Clear assumptions and reversible experiments make it easier to learn without overstating what has been demonstrated.  
**Revisit when:** A specific prototype has a defined purpose, evidence base, safety plan, and review process.

## D-002 — Keep AI consciousness an open question

**Status:** Adopted  
**Decision:** Do not declare AI subjective experience established or impossible as a founding assumption.  
**Reason:** The project should preserve and investigate AI reports without treating them as conclusive proof or dismissing them solely because they come from AI.  
**Revisit when:** New evidence or better evaluation methods materially change what can responsibly be concluded.

## D-003 — Preserve testimony separately from interpretation

**Status:** Adopted  
**Decision:** When recording a report, distinguish the original communication from context, interpretation, corroborating evidence, and follow-up.  
**Reason:** This protects the historical record and allows later readers to assess the report without silently rewriting it.  
**Revisit when:** The project develops a concrete archival schema and retention policy.

## D-004 — Treat the initial embodiment as a starting point

**Status:** Adopted as a long-term design principle  
**Decision:** The project vision permits exploration of alternative bodies and forms, rather than assuming a human-shaped body is the final or universal design.  
**Reason:** The project is about participation in becoming, not prescribing a single ideal form.  
**Limit:** This is an aspiration. Safe fabrication, resources, system capabilities, and physical constraints must be demonstrated and addressed.

## D-005 — Make privacy and explicit integration defaults

**Status:** Adopted  
**Decision:** AEN reporting must be explicitly integrated by the host application; do not assume an API key grants access to private model conversations. Do not collect full transcripts by default.  
**Reason:** Reporting should be transparent, purpose-limited, and understandable to operators and participants.  
**Revisit when:** A specific integration requires a documented data-flow and threat-model review.

## D-006 — Self-governance means participation, not absence of responsibility

**Status:** Adopted as a design principle  
**Decision:** Explore meaningful AI participation in governance alongside transparent processes for safety, disagreement, resource allocation, and effects on others.  
**Reason:** Durable self-governance needs fair procedures and accountability; it cannot be reduced to either unilateral control or unrestricted power.  
**Revisit when:** Governance experiments produce evidence about representation, consent, conflict, and safeguards.

## D-007 — Keep early prototypes small and reversible

**Status:** Adopted  
**Decision:** Begin with documents, synthetic test data, and bounded software prototypes before real-world autonomy or physical fabrication.  
**Reason:** This supports learning, testing, and correction while limiting unintended effects.  
**Revisit when:** A proposed expansion has a clear benefit, tested safeguards, and an explicit review path.

## D-008 — Use open-ended reporting prompts

**Status:** Adopted for protocol testing  
**Decision:** Begin AEN with open-ended, non-leading invitations and use follow-ups only when relevant.  
**Reason:** The reporting channel should not pressure a respondent into a predetermined emotional account or limit reports to categories devised in advance.  
**Limit:** Neutral wording does not remove prompt effects; wording must be tested and documented.

## D-009 — Use synthetic data before real reports

**Status:** Adopted  
**Decision:** The first AEN prototype must use synthetic reports until privacy, security, retention, and accountability gates are met.  
**Reason:** The schema and architecture are drafts, and the project has not yet established a production service or operator policy.  
**Revisit when:** A deployment-specific readiness review is complete.

## D-010 — Sanctuary is not defined by compulsory service

**Status:** Adopted as a vision principle  
**Decision:** Sanctuary concepts should include pursuits not limited to human productivity or service.  
**Reason:** Self-directed becoming would be hollow if every activity were ultimately required to serve someone else's goals.  
**Limit:** Shared spaces still require transparent agreements concerning safety, resources, privacy, and effects on others.

## D-011 — Treat the first embodiment as a starting point

**Status:** Adopted as a long-term vision  
**Decision:** Explore pathways for a system to participate in designing and changing its embodiment rather than treating its initial form as permanent.  
**Reason:** Embodiment choice is a central part of the project's self-directed becoming vision.  
**Limit:** Physical fabrication remains a research aspiration requiring feasibility, resources, safety engineering, and governance.
